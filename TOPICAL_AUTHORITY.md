# Topical Authority — tiang.jalan.co.id

## Role and boundary

`tiang.jalan.co.id` is planned as an Indonesian knowledge and commercial-support property for selecting, specifying, procuring, installing, inspecting, and renewing pole systems used for road lighting, high-mast lighting, CCTV, Wi-Fi/telecom, electricity, traffic signs, lightning protection, and towers. Educational pages own durable decisions and evidence requirements; existing `/tiang/` routes own current product availability, customization, quotation, delivery, and conversion. Dimensions, loads, wind, foundations, electrical safety, materials/coatings, standards/certifications, performance, warranty, and prices require project-specific evidence and qualified review.

## Context used

- The public homepage is a generic Jalan.co.id contractor page, but its navigation explicitly exposes Tiang Jalan, Tower, Lampu Taman, Wi-Fi, Penangkal Petir, PJU, CCTV, High Mast, Rambu, Listrik, and Telkom.
- `sitemap-complete.xml` contains 881 URLs.
- Meaningful routes include the root/context pages plus 11 `/tiang/` hubs: listrik, telkom, PJU, high mast, rambu, CCTV, lampu, tower, Wi-Fi, penangkal petir, and the general tiang hub.
- The sitemap contains 846 location-swapped commercial templates: 94 each for CCTV, high mast, listrik, penangkal petir, PJU, rambu, telkom, tower, and Wi-Fi.
- Evidence gate: the homepage represents the broader parent road contractor while the semantic subdomain, sitemap, and navigation consistently identify poles as this property's role.

## Ignored template noise

- All 846 city/region-swapped product pages were counted as nine template families and excluded from article ideation.
- Category, author, feed, comment, upload/cache, and framework routes/files were excluded.
- Repeated product metadata, page bodies, claims, and location labels were not read.
- General asphalt, concrete, drainage, safety-accessory, and road-construction navigation belongs to the parent business, not this pole taxonomy.
- Exact dimensions, capacity, wind/load, foundation, electrical, coating, standards, certification, warranty, performance, and price claims remain publication/project evidence gates.

## Internal-link rule

Setiap artikel mengarah ke hub topiknya. TJP-01 membagi pembaca ke keluarga produk TJP-07 sampai TJP-14. Seluruh keluarga produk menaut kembali ke TJP-02 sampai TJP-06 untuk geometri, material, coating, load, dan foundation evidence, lalu maju ke TJP-15 dan TJP-16 untuk procurement serta installed lifecycle. Artikel diagnosis menaut ke inspection, safe isolation/escalation, repair, dan replacement decisions. Tautan komersial diberikan hanya setelah equipment, site inputs, interfaces, evidence, dan acceptance criteria jelas.
